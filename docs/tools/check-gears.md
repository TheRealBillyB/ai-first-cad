# `check_gears`

Centre distance, backlash, contact ratio, undercut, and interference for a spur, helical, or bevel pair. A hypoid pair reports offset, ratio, backlash along the tooth, and interference of the face-mill solids through one pinion tooth. A mounted pair uses that placement and clocking. An assembly with a gear train checks each train mesh. Otherwise it checks every gear relation, or every pair of gear instances. One pair that cannot be checked is failed on its own.
