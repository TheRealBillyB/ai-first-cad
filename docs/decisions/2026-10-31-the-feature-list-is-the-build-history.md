# The feature list is the build history

Each shape, sketch, extrude, cut, fillet, and chamfer is a row in order. Choosing a row shows that step's sizes. Changing one rebuilds the steps after it. A step that cannot build is marked with its error, and the solid stays at the last step that could. An older project with no stored history still opens.
