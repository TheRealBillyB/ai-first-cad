# Flanged miniature bearings join the catalog

The ball bearing catalog lists the flanged shielded sizes MF63ZZ, MF74ZZ, MF84ZZ, MF85ZZ, MF95ZZ, MF104ZZ, MF105ZZ, F623ZZ, F694ZZ, F624ZZ, F625ZZ, and F608ZZ. Their bore, outside diameter, shielded width, and flange come from the NMB and NSK miniature and flanged bearing tables. The flange sits on z = 0, and a `flange` mate marks the face that seats on the housing. Thin-section sizes use balls down to 0.5 mm, so a 3 × 6 mm bearing builds.
