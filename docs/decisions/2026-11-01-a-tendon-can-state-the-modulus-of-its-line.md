# A tendon can state the modulus of its line

A braided or laid line is not a bulk material in the handbook library, so `modulus_mpa` on a tendon is used for its elastic elongation over the circular area. `material` is then only the label shown in the report and the bill of materials. Without `modulus_mpa`, the material still has to be a library name.
